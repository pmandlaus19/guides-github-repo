Manage procedure inquiries or changes

Use the available resources to clarify questions or resolve procedure issues, such as inaccuracies or missing information.

Follow these steps to manage procedure inquiries or changes.

  1. Consult with a peer or a manager for assistance.
  2. Utilize available resources in
    • the SalesHub website
    • the OneSource website
    • the Contact List, and
    • the Service Knowledge Hub.
  3. Email the CBCS Field Support Team for assistance with finding or understanding a procedure.
  4. If you are experiencing issues with accessing the procedure library, procedures, or the table of contents, contact the GB Procedures team on Microsoft Teams or email GB Procedures.
  5. If you need to submit procedure changes or report broken links, complete the COS Procedure Writing Intake Form.
