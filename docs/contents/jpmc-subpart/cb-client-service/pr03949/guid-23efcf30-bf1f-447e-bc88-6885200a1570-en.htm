Introduction

The Foreign Draft stop payment requests procedure outlines the pre-requisites and instructions for placing a stop payment on an Foreign Draft. Stop payments cannot be reversed.

Audience

The Client Service team uses the Foreign Draft stop payment requests procedure to review, process, and close stop payment requests.

Foreign drafts

A Foreign Draft is a bank check or a form of payment in physical paper form that is drawn on a financial institution in a currency other than the client's account currency.

Review a Foreign Draft stop payment request

Review a Foreign Draft stop payment request to confirm that the request is valid and the client is legitimate.
PREREQUISITE

PREREQUISITE

Before you begin, open

  • the Authentication Guidelines procedure, and
  • the Foreign Drafts Risk & Process Matrix.

Follow these steps to review a Foreign Draft stop payment request.

  1. Authenticate the requester by following the Authentication Guidelines procedure.
  2. Review the Foreign Drafts Risk & Process Matrix.
    ADDITIONAL INFORMATION:

    The Foreign Drafts Risk & Process Matrix helps to determine

    • the risk rating
    • the authentication method, and
    • if documentation is required.
  3. Gather the information required to complete the request.
    ADDITIONAL INFORMATION:

    The request information includes

    • the client name
    • the FX Draft issue date
    • the account number
    • the reason for the stop payment
    • the FX Draft amount, and
    • the details indicating whether
      • the funds should be reconverted and credited back to the client, or
      • the client wants the draft reissued.

Set a client's expectations on Foreign Draft stop payment requests

Set a client's expectations on Foreign Draft stop payment requests so the client is aware of the turnaround time and the limitations of the Foreign Draft stop payment.

Follow these steps to set a client's expectations on Foreign Draft stop payment requests.

  1. If the client requests to have funds credited back to their account, then set the client's expectation about the credited amount with the assumption that the stop payment is successful.
    1. Inform the client that the funds will be returned to the their account at the current market rate on the credit date.
    2. Inform the client that a lower amount may be credited than what was originally debited because of rate fluctuation.
  2. Provide the timing of the stop payment.
    ADDITIONAL INFORMATION:

    The stop payment may take up to five business days.

Process

Complete the steps to process the request.

  1. Record a Foreign Draft stop payment request
  2. Request a stop payment on a Foreign Draft

Record a Foreign Draft stop payment request

Record a Foreign Draft stop payment request in Navigator for tracking and documentation purposes.

Requirement for client interaction documentation in Navigator

Document client interactions in Navigator using the Navigator case standards, case types and resources procedure.

Follow these steps to record a Foreign Draft stop payment request.

  1. Go to Navigator.
  2. Select an existing case or create a new case.
  3. Select the channel from the Channel dropdown list.
  4. Enter H2H Online in the Sub-Product dropdown list.
    ADDITIONAL INFORMATION:

    STEP RESULT:

    The Case Type field pre-populates and cannot be edited.

  5. Select IR Case Created from the Reason dropdown list.
  6. Select the applicable action from the Action dropdown list if one does not pre-populate.
  7. Enter the summary of the request in the Summary field.
  8. Select SAVE.

Request a stop payment on a Foreign Draft

Request a stop payment on a Foreign Draft so the team in charge of the request can attempt to place a stop on the Foreign Draft.

Follow these steps to request a stop payment on a Foreign Draft.

  1. Contact the team in charge of the stop payment.

    If the Foreign Draft stop payment request is for ...
    Then contact ...

    a Western Hemisphere Emerging Markets (WHEM) based account

    NA FX Payment Investigations to request a stop payment on the FX Draft.

    an APAC based account

    APAC INV to request a stop payment on the FX Draft.

    an EMEA based account

    EMEA Draft – Inquiry and Stop

    a Canada based account

    Canada - Stop Payment on Draft Issued from Access

    a Mexico based account

    Mexico - TRANSACTION ESCALATIONS for MMBSI clients

  2. Provide the request information to the team in charge of the stop payment.

    The request information includes

    • the client name
    • the FX Draft issue date
    • the account number
    • the reason for the stop payment
    • the FX Draft amount, and
    • the details indicating whether
      • the funds should be reconverted and credited back to the client, or
      • the client wants the draft reissued.

  3. Verify that the funds are credited to the client or the FX Draft is re-issued.

    If the funds are ...
    Then ...

    re-converted

    • confirm in eServe that the funds are credited back to the client's account, and
    • notify the client.

    The Service Level Agreement (SLA) to get the funds re-converted and moved between the Nostros accounts and to the client's account can take up to five business days.

    re-issued

    • contact the team in charge of the stop payment to verify that the FX Draft is re-issued, and
    • notify the client.

Wrap-up

The Wrap-up phase of this procedure outlines the pre-requisites and instructions for closing the request.

Close a Foreign Draft stop payment request

Close a Foreign Draft stop payment request to complete the process.
PREREQUISITE

PREREQUISITE

Before you begin, open the CB Client Service Case Management document.

Follow these steps to close a Foreign Draft stop payment request.

  1. Open the case in Navigator.
    1. Confirm the sub-product is correct in the Sub-Product field.
    2. Confirm the reason code is selected from the Reason dropdown list.
      ADDITIONAL INFORMATION:
      Important:

      Confirm IR Case Created is selected from the Reason dropdown list if the request required a Deal Manager implementation request (IR) or a Digital Onboarding request.

  2. Update the client on the status of the case.
    ADDITIONAL INFORMATION:
    Important:

    Encrypt email communications that contain sensitive data.

    See the Information Confidentiality Classifications Standard - Firmwide supplemental document and the Virtru - Email Encryption Solution guide in Confluence.

  3. Ask the client to confirm if they view their request to be complete.
  4. Select Pending Confirmation in the Status dropdown list.
    ADDITIONAL INFORMATION:

    Step result: The case automatically closes if a response is not received after two business days.

  5. Enter a complete description of the steps taken to resolve the client's inquiry in the Resolution field.
  6. Review the CB Client Service Case Management document before closing the case.
